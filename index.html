01 / INTRODUCTION

About Me

Hello! I’m a computer science student with a strong interest in mathematics, origami, guzheng, and Peking Opera (京剧). I love learning how things work, exploring new ideas, and finding creative ways to approach challenges. Computer science interests me because it brings together logical thinking, problem-solving, and the opportunity to build something useful from an idea.

Mathematics inspires me to recognize patterns, think carefully, and work through challenging questions. I also enjoy origami, where a simple sheet of paper can become something intricate through imagination, precision, and patience. Both interests remind me that complex results often begin with small, thoughtful steps.

Outside of academics, I’m interested in traditional Chinese arts. I enjoy the guzheng, a traditional Chinese string instrument, and Peking Opera (京剧), a performing art that combines singing, acting, movement, and storytelling. These interests give me another way to explore creativity and connect with Chinese culture.

I created this portfolio to introduce myself and gradually document my learning journey. As I develop computer science projects, strengthen my skills, and create new work, I’ll share them here. I hope this space will show both what I’m interested in today and how my interests grow over time. Looking ahead, I dream of founding a company focused on data and creating a Peking Opera troupe that celebrates and shares this art form.
